The safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, in one line:

> WARNING: Contains one human. Prone to sudden silence after remembering something embarrassing from 2009.

Why this label: it opens with real label wording ("WARNING: Contains one human"), then names a hazard every reader recognises in themselves — going quiet after a decade-old embarrassment resurfaces. The specific year (2009) is what makes it land instead of reading generic.
